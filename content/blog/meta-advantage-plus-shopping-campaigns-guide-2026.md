## What Are Meta Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns?

Meta Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns are AI-powered sales campaigns that automatically optimize audience targeting, placements, bidding, and ad delivery to generate more purchases with minimal manual intervention.

Unlike traditional Facebook campaigns where advertisers create multiple audiences and ad sets, Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns consolidate everything into one intelligent campaign.

Meta continuously analyzes thousands of signals including:

- Purchase history
- User behavior
- Device usage
- Shopping intent
- Engagement history
- Website activity
- Pixel events
- Conversion probability

The system then delivers your ads to people who are most likely to convert.

Think of it as having an experienced media buyer making thousands of optimization decisions every second.

## When You Should Avoid Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns

Although powerful, ASC isn't perfect.

It may not be suitable if:

- Your account has very little conversion data.
- You're generating fewer than 20 conversions monthly.
- Your pixel isn't tracking purchases correctly.
- You're running awareness campaigns.
- You're promoting local services instead of products.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns better than manual campaigns?

For many eCommerce businesses, yes. Meta's AI often identifies converting audiences more efficiently than manual targeting. However, results depend on proper tracking, quality creatives, and a well-optimized website.

### Do I still need interest targeting?

Not necessarily. Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns primarily rely on Meta's machine learning, reducing the need for extensive manual interest targeting.

### Can I use Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns without a product catalog?

While they're designed for shopping campaigns, Meta continues to expand support for different sales objectives. For the best experience, a properly configured product catalog is recommended.

### How long should I let the campaign run before making changes?

Allow at least 5–7 days, or until the campaign exits the learning phase, before making significant optimizations.

### Is Meta Pixel required?

Yes. For optimal performance, use both Meta Pixel and Conversion API so Meta receives complete conversion data.
